Privacy Policy

Leading Edge Consulting · Internal QuickBooks Reporting Integration
Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Leading Edge Consulting ("we", "us") handles data in connection with our internal QuickBooks reporting integration (the "Software"). The Software is private and internal. It is used only by our own authorized personnel, connects only to our own QuickBooks Online company file, and is not offered to the public or to any third party.

2. Data We Access

Through the Intuit QuickBooks Online Accounting API, the Software reads business accounting records belonging to us, which may include:

  • Invoice records, including amounts, dates, and payment status
  • Payment and receipt records associated with those invoices
  • Customer records associated with those invoices, including business name and contact details
  • Company profile information for our own QuickBooks company

The Software performs read operations for reporting purposes. It does not process, transmit, or settle payment transactions, and it does not access or store cardholder data or bank account numbers.

3. How We Use Data

Accessed data is used for one purpose: generating internal business reports on invoice and payment status. We do not sell, rent, share, or otherwise disclose this data to any third party. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any secondary purpose.

4. Storage and Retention

Report outputs and any cached API responses are stored on infrastructure we control, accessible only to authorized personnel. Data is retained only as long as needed for current reporting and is deleted when no longer required or upon disconnection of the integration.

5. OAuth Credentials

The Software stores OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens issued by Intuit in order to maintain its connection. Tokens are stored with restricted access, are never transmitted to third parties, and are destroyed when the integration is disconnected.

6. Security

We protect accessed data using access controls limiting use to authorized personnel, encryption in transit (TLS) for all API communication, restricted storage of credentials, and prompt revocation of access when no longer needed.

7. Third Parties

The Software transmits data only between our own systems and Intuit's QuickBooks Online API. No other third-party service receives data accessed through the integration.

8. Your Rights

Because the Software processes only our own company's business records and has no external end users, there are no third-party data subjects with rights arising from its operation. Where customer contact details appear within our accounting records, they are handled under our general business privacy practices and applicable law.

9. Disconnection

The integration may be disconnected at any time from within QuickBooks Online or by revoking the application's access. On disconnection, we cease API access and destroy stored tokens.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy. The current version is always available at this URL.
